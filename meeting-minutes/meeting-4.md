## Preparation for the First Client Meeting

The team discussed the questions and requirements that should be clarified during the first meeting with the client.

### Application Design

The team will ask the client how they envision the application and its user interface. If possible, the client will be asked to provide a basic sketch or example showing how they expect the application to look and function.

### Skeletal SVGs

The team will ask whether the client will provide the required skeletal SVGs or whether the team is expected to source or create them.

### Colour-Coded Bone States

The team will ask which bone states must be represented through colour coding. Possible examples include:

* Present
* Absent
* Pathological
* Other states specified by the client

## Requirements Requiring Clarification from last meeting

### Feature One

No further clarification was considered necessary at this stage.

### Feature Two — Skeletal Variations

The application may need to switch between different skeletal SVGs according to age categories, including:

* Infant
* Child
* Adolescent
* Adult

The team will also confirm whether separate skeletal representations are required for different sexes.

Thisen will research the availability of suitable skeletal SVGs for the required age categories and sexes.

### Feature Three — Clickable Areas

The team will ask how precisely each skeleton must be divided into clickable areas. This includes whether users should select:

* Entire bones
* Specific regions of individual bones
* Smaller anatomical zones, such as joints and shafts

### Feature Four — Exporting Records

The following export requirements require clarification:

* The required export format, such as CSV, JSON, images or another format
* Whether the export should include a written summary, images or both
* Whether users should export the entire skeleton or select individual bones
* Whether exported records must retain all colour-coded bone states and associated metadata

The team will also explain the limitations of browser-based storage and clarify the required level of data persistence.
